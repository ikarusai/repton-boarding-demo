                                          One thing I can do next

                                          Try this before a lesson, a match, or a difficult conversation.

                                          1. Pause for a moment and notice what’s on your mind.

                                          Delivery and resources

                                          Running the house programme

                                          Plan 20–30 minutes for the weekly house session. Use fictional situations and give boarders the option to reflect privately. Add practical workshops and house challenges monthly, and review the programme with student feedback each term.

                                          For individual concerns or sensitive topics, use Repton’s approved pastoral and safeguarding arrangements.

                                          • Anna Freud classroom toolkit ↗Activities for supportive relationships, belonging and emotional wellbeing.
                                          • UNICEF Helping Adolescents Thrive ↗Resources for planning adolescent wellbeing activities.
                                          • Barclays LifeSkills budget game ↗Use for a workshop and adapt the prices and scenarios to Dubai.
                                          • Gatsby careers guidance ↗Planning reference for strengths, careers conversations and transitions.
                                          • KHDA Skills for Life ↗Dubai schools’ programme planning reference.
                                          • UAE child protection policy ↗Staff reference alongside Repton’s procedures.

                                          I want to talk to someone

                                          Choose who you’d feel comfortable talking to.

                                          • Boarding teamFind a member of the Fry House boarding team and ask to speak in private.
                                          • School counsellorAsk your house tutor to help you arrange a chat.
                                          • School safeguarding leadTell a trusted adult if you or someone else feels unsafe.

                                          For a private conversation today, speak to one of the adults above. The anonymous inbox is being set up.

                                          Ask a question anonymously

                                          This is a preview of the anonymous question process.

                                          1. Ask your question. You can leave out names and details that identify you or anyone else.
                                          2. Save your reply code and use it to check back.

                                          If a teacher thinks someone may be unsafe, they follow the school’s safeguarding process.

                                          You can tell a trusted adult even if you’re unsure what to say.
